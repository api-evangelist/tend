An overjet is the horizontal space between your upper and lower front teeth, and having one is completely normal. But when that space is larger than usual, it can affect your bite, your teeth and sometimes your confidence in your smile. Here’s what causes an excessive overjet, how it differs from an overbite and what treatment can do to help.
